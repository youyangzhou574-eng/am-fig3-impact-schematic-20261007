## Original-source facts

The original manuscript, sections 2.3 and Drop-Weight Impact Measurements, gives a 370 g impactor released from 10, 20 and 30 cm. It specifies a circuit-free circular specimen, diameter 74 mm and total thickness 6 mm. The liquid and hybrid layered configurations comprise a 1 mm base, 4.5 mm intermediate region and 0.5 mm cover. It locates the force sensor at the rear side of the impactor.

The historical schematic labels a part below the sample as Force sensor and the upper module as Accelerator. These labels conflict with the original manuscript. This model uses the original text: the rear/top module is the force sensor, and the lower metal plate is the rigid support. It does not add a separate acceleration instrument unsupported by the text.
