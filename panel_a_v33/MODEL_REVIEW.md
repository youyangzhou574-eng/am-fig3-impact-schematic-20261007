# Inverted trapezoid orange downward impact trail

The human identified the previous upper-narrow / lower-wide cue as resembling upward flames and explicitly requested an inverted trapezoid. This revision reverses the taper: wider above, narrower at the lower impact contact.

![Matching preview](05_impact_alignment_v33_preview_white.png)

The upper nominal width is 22 mm and the lower nominal width remains 16 mm, matching the central indentation. The short 12 mm screen-plane height, original orange shader, registered camera and central bottom anchor are retained. The side curves are mild, the lower corners have only 0.25 mm rounding, and the broad upper part fades smoothly. Longitudinal streaks were removed; the cue is one continuous gradient.
