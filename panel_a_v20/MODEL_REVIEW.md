# Cool coordinated palette and a three-dimensional pillar close-up

The human requested a better coordinated palette and a clearer three-dimensional appearance, while retaining the milky SSG, shallow arc waves and translucent top cover.

![Three-dimensional SSG-pillar close-up](03_pillar_context_v20_preview_white.png)

[PNG close-up asset](03_pillar_context_v20_asset.png)

Foreground silicone pillars now use a cool slate-blue display color, the SSG is pale milky ice-blue, the surrounding array is lighter blue-gray, and the base/support use neutral cool grays. Colors distinguish visual roles, not different physical silicone materials. Directional studio light and subdued fill/backlight provide depth and material highlights. A normal-only silicone bevel softens edge lighting without changing geometry.

The wider 160 mm perspective view uses a 26-degree elevation and 38-degree side angle. This retains the section as the primary face while revealing triangular pillar tops, the side faces of the surrounding prisms, layer thickness and array depth. The earlier almost-frontal view hid the rear-pointing prism sides behind the section face; lighting or color alone could not expose them. Camera angles and optical settings are illustration choices, not source dimensions.

The complete nominal 0.5 mm silicone/PDMS cover is retained at 0.18 display opacity. The shared y=1.70 mm section still exposes the opaque foreground pillar faces directly, with no SSG layer in front of them. The enclosure remains filled with turbid SSG around and behind the gently bowed pillars. The 0.25 mm midpoint bow and all source meshes and shape keys are unchanged. Three soft, shallow arc-wave highlights remain in the liquid material, with no dense noise texture, arrows, leaders or rendered text.

The shallow waves are qualitative static mechanism cues, not a physical open free surface or a computed velocity, particle, stress or fluid field. No physics solver was run. Tint, transparency, scattering, wave highlights and light settings are not measured material data.

27 native checks passed after reopening: source mesh geometry and shape keys match the previous version; the shared section has opaque bare pillar caps; camera rays hit each solid cap before any SSG; 30 section points remain filled by either SSG or silicone without sampled overlap; the top cover is retained; the perspective, palette and native shallow-wave shader are present; and the silicone bevel affects shading only.
